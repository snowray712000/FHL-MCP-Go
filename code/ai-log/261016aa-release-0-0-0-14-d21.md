## What
Per the user's choice (30 verses, refuse instead of truncate): one `get_parsing_reference` request may cover at most 30 verses (list, `end_verse` and cross-chapter ranges all count). Over the limit -> `Invalid parameter 'verse': <input> - the range covers more than 30 verses; get_parsing_reference allows at most 30 verses per request, split it into smaller requests`, raised before any `qp.php` request.

## How
- `expandVerseString` and `seq` compare `to-from` against the remaining room before looping (no allocation for `1-9223372036854775807`; the `b-a < 0` check catches int overflow).
- Cross-chapter: a span of >= 30 chapters is refused without chapter-length lookups (each chapter has >= 1 verse); otherwise lookups proceed chapter by chapter with the remaining room.
- Error is framed on `verse` for every path (also when the excess comes from `end_verse`/`end_chapter`) - assumption.
